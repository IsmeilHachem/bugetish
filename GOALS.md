# BudgetIsh — Goals & Vision

## What it is
An all-in-one personal finance tracker built to help me stay on top of my money,
track transactions, and work toward having this month's paychecks cover next month's bills.

## Who it's for
Primarily me. Secondarily, my sister — who can run her own local version when it's ready to share.

## The one thing it has to do well
Track balance and transactions accurately. Everything else is secondary.

## Why I'm building it
Not for money. Not for a portfolio. For my actual life.
This app is a tool I need to work — because the goal behind it is financial stability.

## What "done enough" looks like
The current version is already there. What comes next is refinement and access:
- A live URL I can save to my phone's home screen
- Hosted cheaply (ideally free or near-free)
- Always available, always up to date

## How I'm building it
Vibe coding — describing what I want in plain language and shipping fast with AI.
The skill I'm building is knowing how to take an idea and turn it into a working product.

## 12-month goal
Having this month's paychecks paying next month's bills.
BudgetIsh is the tool that helps me see whether I'm on track.

## Next steps (in order)
1. Keep entering transactions consistently — the app only works if the data is in it
2. Deploy to a live URL (Vercel or Netlify — free tier, no ongoing cost)
3. Add it to phone home screen as a PWA
4. Improve and extend features through vibe coding sessions
